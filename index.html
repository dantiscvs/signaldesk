How it works

From publication to your Slack
in under two minutes

A fully automated pipeline monitors official sources on a two-minute cycle, extracts meaningful content, scores it, and fires only the signals that meet your threshold.

01 / 03
📡
Monitor
RSS and web crawlers watch official publication endpoints every two minutes, deduplicating against a persistent store so each item is processed exactly once.
→
02 / 03
🧠
Extract & Score
An AI triage layer reads the full article, writes a single factual one-liner with key figures and direction, assigns a significance score 0–100, and flags whether the content is novel enough to escalate.
→
03 / 03
⚡
Deliver
Signals that meet your personal significance threshold and source filters are pushed to your Slack channel — with the one-liner, score, grounding status, and a direct link to the source.
